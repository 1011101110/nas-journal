title: "Feeding in the NICU"
description: "Getting calories in when sucking is dysregulated and everything takes effort."
stage: newborn
order: 2
---

Feeding was a battle I didn't expect. I'd read about breastfeeding challenges but nothing prepared me for a baby whose suck-swallow-breathe reflex was completely dysregulated.

He'd latch, suck twice, and fall asleep. Or he'd suck frantically, take in too much air, and vomit it all back. The lactation consultant was kind but honest: this might not work the way I'd hoped.

We ended up with a combination — expressed milk in a slow-flow bottle when he was too weak to nurse, nursing when he had enough energy. Some days the goal was just calories in, however that happened.

Tips that actually helped:
- **Paced bottle feeding** — holding the bottle horizontal, letting him work for each swallow
- **Burping constantly** — not just at the end, but every few sucks
- **Feed before he's frantic** — NAS babies escalate fast, and a screaming baby can't eat
- **Keep the room dim and quiet** — feeding is hard enough without extra sensory input

The NICU nurses were my education. I asked them everything.
